2. Cashback and Rewards Apps: Save and Earn!

Who doesn’t like saving money and still earning extra? These apps give you cashback or points that can be exchanged for money, gift cards, or discounts every time you shop at partner stores. 3. Product and Service Selling Apps: Turn What You Have Into Profit!

Got something to sell? A skill to offer? These apps are your digital showcase!:

  • Creating Your Online Store: Platforms like Shopify allow you to create your own e-commerce store and sell new, handmade products or even work with dropshipping (selling without stock).

4. Investment Apps: Make Your Money Work for You!

Platforms like NuInvest, Rico, and XP Investimentos have made the investment world accessible to everyone. Remember: the best time to start investing is now!

5. Delivery and Transportation Apps: Be Your Own Driver or Delivery Person!

If you have a vehicle (car, motorcycle, bike) and available time, apps like Uber, 99, iFood, and Rappi offer a flexible way to make money online by delivering or transporting passengers.
