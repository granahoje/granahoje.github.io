Why invest monthly?

The secret to wealth is not how much you earn, but how much you save and invest consistently.

  • Creates the habit of financial discipline.
  • Takes advantage of dollar-cost averaging (DCA).
  • Powers compound interest in the long run.
  • Allows starting with low amounts.
