How does LOCS work?

LOCS calculates the impact of a non-essential expense on your journey to financial freedom. It considers your income, essential expenses, and the real rate of return on your investments to quantify the additional work time needed to offset the expense, or how much your financial freedom is delayed.

Why use LOCS?

This innovative tool helps you make more conscious consumption decisions, transforming the monetary value of an expense into a more tangible metric: your time and future freedom. It's a powerful way to visualize the true opportunity cost of your lifestyle.
