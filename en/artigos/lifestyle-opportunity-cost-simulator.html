The Methodology Behind LOCS: EEAT in Action

Our approach to LOCS is deeply rooted in the principles of **Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, and Trustworthiness (EEAT)**. It's not about a superficial calculation, but a model that integrates concepts from behavioral finance, time economics, and market projections. The Grana Hoje team, composed of economists, financial planners, and psychologists, worked to create a tool that not only calculates but educates and transforms.

The LOCS algorithm considers your net monthly income, essential expenses, current savings rate, and a real rate of return on your investments. By entering the value of a non-essential expense, it projects how much longer you would need to work to offset that expense, or how many years of financial freedom you are delaying. It's a reality check, but a necessary one for those seeking total control of their lives.

"Every dollar spent today on something you don't need is one less minute of freedom tomorrow."

How LOCS Transforms Your Consumption Decisions

Imagine you're thinking of buying a new smartphone for $1,000. LOCS won't tell you not to buy it. It will show you that, with your income and savings rate, this expense represents, for example, 3 additional months of work or 6 fewer months of early retirement. With this information, the decision becomes much more conscious.

  1. Expanded Awareness: You start to see every non-essential expense as a trade-off for time and freedom.
  2. Smart Prioritization: Helps differentiate what is truly important from what is just a fleeting desire.
  3. Lasting Motivation: Visualizing the long-term impact strengthens your financial discipline.
